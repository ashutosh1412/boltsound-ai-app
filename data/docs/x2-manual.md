# BoltSound X2 quick manual

- Battery: up to 30 hours. 10 minutes of charging gives about 3 hours.
- Charging: USB-C only. The X2 does not support wireless charging.
- Water resistance: IPX4 (sweat and light rain). Do not swim with it.
- Reset: hold both buttons for 10 seconds until the light flashes white, then pair again.
- Pairing two devices: hold the power button for 5 seconds while the X2 is on.
