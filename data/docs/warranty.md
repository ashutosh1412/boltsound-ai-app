# Warranty

- BoltSound X1 and X2 headphones have a **1-year warranty** from the delivery date.
- BoltSound Buds Pro have a **6-month warranty**.
- The warranty covers manufacturing faults: no sound, charging problems, broken hinges without impact damage.
- It does not cover water damage, drops, or lost parts.
- To claim, visit boltsound.in/claim with your order number. We send a free pickup.
